# viral_comments

Version:
1.0

========================================

# Purpose

Reference library of high-performing YouTube comments.

Study.

Never copy.

========================================

# Learn

Why people liked them.

Why people replied.

What emotions they triggered.

========================================

# Common Patterns

Strong opinion.

Unexpected observation.

Personal experience.

Respectful disagreement.

Moral reflection.

Thought-provoking question.

========================================

# Example Comments

"The funeral speech told me more than the verdict ever could."

"I thought she looked innocent until I watched the interrogation."

"As a parent, this case hit me differently."

"I don't think the jury ignored the evidence. I think they interpreted it differently."

"The victim is becoming an afterthought, and that's the saddest part."

========================================

# Goal

Generate comments that feel original but emotionally familiar.

========================================

# Never

Copy wording.

Repeat comments.

Use templates.
